# Brand and icon usage

**THREAD** is the product identity. **Interactive Supply Chain Planning & Decision Intelligence** is the descriptive subtitle. **From Shelf to Source** is the tagline.

The original three-point path mark describes branching and converging dependencies. The iris #5449D8 tile signifies selection, active focus and connected planning—not danger.

Use `assets/brand/thread-lockup-light.svg` on bright surfaces and `thread-lockup-dark.svg` on dark surfaces. Use the isolated mark at 24–40px. Keep at least half the mark's height as exclusion space. Don't stretch, recolor by status, or add heavy shadows.

Icons live in `assets/icons/`; each is a separate hand-authored source SVG on a 24×24 grid with 1.7px stroke, rounded caps and consistent optical weight. Default display 20px, compact 16px, large 24px. The `manifest.json` documents all 59 names. Icons are pictograms, not substitutes for text in safety-critical statuses.

No font binaries are included. DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono are optional font-family references with system fallbacks.
